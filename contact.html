What Happens When You Call?

You’ll reach someone who actually works on floors, not a call center. Tell us the type of space (garage, basement, commercial), the approximate square footage, and whether you’ve noticed any cracks, stains, or moisture issues. We’ll ask a few follow-up questions and either give you a rough range over the phone or schedule a free on-site visit to give you an exact number.

On-site estimates take about 20 minutes. We bring a moisture meter, measure the slab, document any cracks or problem areas, and walk you through the options before we quote anything. There’s no deposit required to get an estimate and no obligation to book.
